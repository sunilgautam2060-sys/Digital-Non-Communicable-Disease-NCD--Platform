PRIORITY CONDITIONS

Focused On Three Key Health Areas

NCD Nepal focuses on three important non-communicable health conditions.

01

Diabetes

Know, test and manage your blood glucose and understand healthy lifestyle practices.

  • Understand diabetes
  • Monitor blood glucose
  • Improve healthy habits
02

Blood Pressure

Check your blood pressure regularly and learn how to maintain healthy levels.

  • Check blood pressure
  • Control risk factors
  • Follow up regularly
03

Thyroid

Learn about thyroid health, screening, treatment and ongoing monitoring.

  • Understand thyroid health
  • Screen when appropriate
  • Monitor regularly
